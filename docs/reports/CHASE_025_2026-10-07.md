## 1 · What the gate is (read, not remembered)


Caller `backend/v9/gateway/trading_gateway.py:2847-3103`: VAH/VAL come from Sierra `tpo.json` (`_load_sierra_tpo`), skipped on session immaturity (<2 closed bars or leg < floor, T-242), skipped by the leaf's `skip_gates` (T-480 `take_with_extension`), by `OPENING_DRIVE_BRANCH_V1` (OFF) and `VAR_CONT_V1` (OFF). The block lands as `blocked_by="entry_location_quality"`, `reason="beyond_value: ex=X.XX > 0.25 …"` — so the recorded replay carries the exact `ex` per blocked candidate. `chaser` can co-occur in the same reason string; `expensive_stop` is shadow (10.09) and never blocks.

## 2 · Method — candidate-level score, no new harness

Source: the 65 recorded sessions of the live-config replay `harness_out/t466/t529b_*.json` (`routes[]` = every setup the producers emitted, with `blocked_by/reason`, `entry/stop/t1/t2/t3`, `il`, `tree_v3`). Bars: the session's real 5-min bars from `v9_bars_5min_woodies` (read-only, `default_transaction_read_only=on`), ET date = session, `< 16:00 ET` — the same window the harness settles on. Script: `harness_out/t24/chase025_score.py` (copy of the one-shot), output `harness_out/t24/chase025_t529b.out`.

Selection: `blocked_by == entry_location_quality` **and** the reason has `beyond_value` **and not** `chaser` (so the only reason the candidate died was `ex > 0.25`). Result: **324 routes over 60 sessions — 100 % of all ELQ blocks in t529b are beyond_value-only** (no chaser blocks at all in this replay).

Scoring = the harness `_settle_bar` semantics, simplified: 3 contracts (`RISK_MIN_CONTRACTS=3` in the replay flags), legs C1→t1 · C2→t2 · C3→t3 (t3 missing → follows t2; both missing → runner to EOD), fill at entry if inside the entry bar else at its open, conservative stop-before-target on the same bar, stop→BE after T1, EOD flatten at the last `<16:00 ET` close, $5/pt, commission 2.60$/round-trip. Tree-leaf `exit {t1_r,t2_r}` would override the ladder — none of the 324 carries one. 24 candidates with a degenerate ladder (a target on the wrong side of the entry — repaired live by `_seed_runner_targets`/`_target_degeneracy_guard`, not reproduced here) and 2 without a matching bar are excluded and counted.

**What this is not:** it is not "what the P&L would have been with the gate off". The downstream chain (live slot, pattern cooldown, daily caps, direction-conflict, sizing) is not replayed, and many of the 324 are the same bar fired by 2–4 producers (ZLR + REACTIVE + CONFLUENCE_RI_ZLR + DOUBLE_BOTTOM at one price). It is the per-candidate **upper bound of what admitting them could add** — the same convention as the brief's "+1,085$ ברמת-מועמד" for `extreme_chase_guard`. A negative upper bound is decisive; a positive one would only have been a reason to run the harness.

## 4 · Reading

1. **The gate is doing its job.** 298 candidates the live tree said TAKE and only `beyond_value` stopped: 50 % win rate but −13.0k$ gross, i.e. the losers are much bigger than the winners (chased entries with the stop back inside value: INITIATIVE_LONG −3.7k$ on 33, INITIATIVE_SHORT −3.3k$ on 38, ZLR SHORT −3.4k$ on 57). This is exactly the 28.08 ruling ("late location is why we lost") measured.
2. **The nearest bucket is the worst place to loosen.** `ex ∈ (0.25, 0.50]` — the only range a "small" raise to 0.50 would admit — is 133 candidates for **−3,052$** (69 wins). There is no soft edge just above 0.25.
3. **The one positive pocket is the opening, not a threshold.** Everything with `ex > 1.5` at 16:45–17:00 IL (VA width of a 3-bar session: CEILING_FLIP_TOUCH2, FAILED_BREAK, OPENING_ORR/EXTREME_REJECT) is the T-457 `OPENING_DRIVE_BRANCH_V1` question (flag OFF, pending ruling), already catalogued in `trading_gateway.py:2866-2883`; it is not an `ex_max` question and is not re-opened here. FAILED_BREAK_LONG +990$ (6) and CEILING_FLIP_LONG +728$ (5) are the only producers in the green and both are N<10 (brief (ו): N not sufficient).
4. **Doctrine check (Dalton):** `beyond_value` for a with-trend phase-D entry is already exempted in code (`_elq_skip_d`, Michael 17.09) and for `take_with_extension` by the leaf (T-480). The 20 phase-D candidates here are the ones that were *not* with the playbook bias; they net −66$ (flat), so phase D is not where the gate's value sits. Per phase: **C 200 → −11,826$** · B 76 → −698$ · D 20 → −66$ · A 2 → −450$ (`harness_out/t24/chase025_phaseD.out`, `PHASE_SUMS`, same scorer). The money the gate saves is phase C — exactly the rotation-hours chase the 28.08 ruling described. Nothing to add.
