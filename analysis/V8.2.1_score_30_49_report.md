# V8.2.1 Score 30-49 Bucket Investigation
## Date: 2026-05-01 | Dataset: ~3,000 attempts + ~380 closed setups

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Score 30-49 bucket's superior WR is a **direction artifact**, not a scoring quality signal. Score 70+ setups are 100% LONG (because Vegas BULLISH = 30pts pushes score above 70). Score 30-49 setups are 100% SHORT (because Vegas opposes SHORT = 0pts, capping score below 50). During the observation period, SHORT setups outperformed LONG in aggregate, making the "low score" bucket appear to win more. The quality score is functioning as a **direction proxy**, not an independent quality measure.

## 4. Component Pattern Analysis

### Why Score 70+ loses (LONG setups)

Score 70+ requires Vegas match (30pts). These are LONG setups where:
- Vegas trend = BULLISH (EMA 144/169 slopes up)
- But market is at/near reversal point
- STOP rate: 62% (62 of 100)
- When they win, they win BIG (T2 hits: 24 of 100)

Pattern: "Right trend, wrong timing" — Vegas is 7-8 hour lagging indicator.

### Why Score 30-49 wins more (SHORT setups)

Score 30-49 gets 0 from Vegas (opposes direction), then accumulates:
- TPO: 12-25pts (position-based, not directional lag)
- FVG: 15-25pts (recent gaps exist)
- Footprint: 0-20pts (delta confirms or opposes)

These setups trade AGAINST Vegas trend, which often means they catch reversals.

### Sample winning 30-49 pattern:
- Vegas = 0 (BULLISH opposes SHORT)
- TPO = 12-18 (price below POC, or in VA)
- FVG = 15 (1-2 recent bearish FVGs)
- Footprint = 4-14 (moderate delta confirms)
- Total: 31-47

### Sample losing 70+ pattern:
- Vegas = 30 (BULLISH matches LONG)
- TPO = 18-25 (price above POC, in VA)
- FVG = 15-25 (multiple bullish FVGs accumulated during uptrend)
- Footprint = 8-14 (delta confirms buying)
- Total: 71-94
- Reality: buying at the TOP of a move, everything "confirms" but price reverses

## 5. Three Testable Hypotheses

### H1: Vegas Score = 0 is POSITIVE for WR (Vegas as Contrarian)

**Claim:** Setups where Vegas opposes direction (vegas_score=0) have higher WR than setups where Vegas confirms (vegas_score=30), controlling for direction.

**Test:** Compare SHORT setups with vegas_score=0 vs SHORT setups with vegas_score>0 (if any exist). If H1 is true, the WR gap disappears or inverts when direction is held constant.

**Falsification:** If SHORT setups with partial Vegas credit (vegas_score=9-15, when trend is NEUTRAL) have HIGHER WR than those with vegas_score=0, then Vegas signal has some value even for opposing direction.

### H2: FVG Count > 5 = Exhaustion Signal (Stale Accumulation)

**Claim:** When score_reasons shows "FVG bullish: 13 matches" (high count), the setup is entering near trend exhaustion. FVG gaps accumulate during a move and the high count marks the END, not the beginning.

**Test:** Parse score_reasons for FVG match count. Split into count<=3 vs count>5. Compare WR within same score bucket and direction.

**Falsification:** If high FVG count setups have SAME or BETTER WR than low count, then FVG count is neutral and the issue is elsewhere.

### H3: Score Anti-Correlation Disappears When Controlling for Direction

**Claim:** The anti-correlation (higher score = lower WR) is entirely a confound of direction distribution. Within same-direction setups, score IS predictive (or at least neutral).

**Test:** Filter to SHORT-only setups. Check if 30-49 SHORT still beats 50-69 SHORT. Expected: WR should be similar (40% vs 53% — but 50-69 SHORT actually WINS more, supporting this hypothesis).

**Falsification:** If the anti-correlation persists within same-direction subsets, then the score is genuinely anti-predictive beyond the direction effect.

**Preliminary evidence FOR H3:** 
- Score 50-69 SHORT: 52.9% WR (n=34)
- Score 30-49 SHORT: 40.0% WR (n=65)
- Higher score SHORT WINS more! The anti-correlation is REVERSED within direction.

## 6. Recommended A/B Test for Phase 3.3

### Primary test: Vegas Direction-Agnostic Scoring

**Control (A):** Current scoring — Vegas gives 0/15/30 based on trend match.

**Treatment (B):** Vegas scores tunnel WIDTH only (direction-agnostic):
- Width >= 2.0pt: 30pts ("clear structure")
- Width 0.5-2.0: 20pts ("moderate structure")
- Width < 0.5: 10pts ("unclear")

**Metric:** WR within direction, controlling for score bucket.

**Expected outcome:** Score becomes direction-neutral. Anti-correlation disappears. Score becomes weakly positive predictor.

**Duration:** 3 days of parallel scoring (both A and B computed, only A used for execution). Compare bucket WR under both formulas.

---

## 7. Key Insight for Quality Score v2

The current score doesn't measure "setup quality." It measures "does this setup agree with a lagging trend indicator." Agreement with a lagging indicator at turning points = maximum confidence at the worst possible time.

**The fix is NOT to lower Vegas weight. It's to make Vegas score direction-agnostic** (measure tunnel structure/width, not trend match). This preserves Vegas as a "market clarity" indicator while removing the directional bias that creates the anti-correlation.

The preliminary evidence (H3) suggests that within same-direction subsets, higher component scores (TPO + FVG + Footprint) DO predict better outcomes. The scoring framework is sound — only the Vegas direction-matching component is broken.
