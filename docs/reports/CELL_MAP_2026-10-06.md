## 1. Data, windows, and mechanical rules used

| Item | Source / definition |
|---|---|
| Bars | `v9_bars_5min_woodies`, `symbol='MES'`. `ts` = **bar open time** (verified: the 16:30 IL bar is the RTH open; bars 16:30..17:25 reproduce `v9_tpo_sessions.ib_high/ib_low` on 2026-10-02 to the tick). |
| Time zone | All clock times **IL (Asia/Jerusalem)**, as in the order. Over the whole sample both IL and New York are on DST, so 16:30 / 17:30 / 20:00 IL ≡ 09:30 / 10:30 / 13:00 ET exactly (fixed 7 h). |
| IB | **bar-math** `max(high)/min(low)` over the 12 bars 16:30..17:25 IL. `v9_tpo_sessions` CASH `ib_high/ib_low` was used only as a cross-check: it agrees within 1 tick on 70/75 sessions and is **wrong on 5** (06-12, 07-10, 07-15, 07-17, 07-21 — e.g. 07-17 tpo `7539/7473` vs bars `7533.75/7504.75`). Rule 2 → bar-math wins. |
| VA (Normal fade) | **prior CASH session** `vah/val/poc` from `v9_tpo_sessions` (the only VA known at 17:30; same-day VA would be look-ahead). Accepted only if `val ≤ poc ≤ vah`, `vah > val`, and the VA sits inside the prior day's RTH range ±5 pt. Rejected: 2 (outside range) + 4 (prior day has no bars). |
| Phase C | entry bars 17:30..19:50 IL; **exit = close of the 19:55 bar** (the last bar that closes by 20:00). One session (08-04) is missing the 19:55 bar → exit at the 19:50 close, flagged in the appendix. |
| Drive net | `close(17:25 bar) − open(16:30 bar)` — the 16:30–17:30 IL net per the order. |
| Bias | OPEN_DRIVE / OPEN_TEST_DRIVE → sign(net). OPEN_REJECTION_REVERSE → −sign(net). OPEN_AUCTION_IN / OUT / INDETERMINATE → none. A trade is tagged `with` / `against` / `none` by its side vs bias. |
| Stop vs target on one bar | conservative: **stop checked first**, on the entry bar too. |

Playbook cells as scored (Michael's phase-C rules, made mechanical):

| day_type | trade | entry | stop | exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trend_Normal / Trend_DD | WITH the day's net (long if close 20:00 > open 17:30, else short) | first counter-colored bar after 17:30, at its close | 1 tick beyond that bar's low/high | 20:00 |
| Normal | fade VAH (short) / VAL (long), each once | first bar whose range contains the level, filled at the level | 2 pt beyond | target prior POC, else 20:00 |
| Variation / Normal_Variation | WITH the first close beyond IB (one trade) | that close | 1 tick back inside the IB edge | 20:00 |
| Neutral_Center / Neutral_Extreme | fade IBH (short) / IBL (long), each once | first bar whose range contains the level | 2 pt beyond | IB mid, else 20:00 |
| Nontrend / Nonconviction | none | | | |

⚠ **Trend direction uses hindsight.** "long if close 20:00 > open 17:30" is decided with the 20:00 close, so the Trend Σ$ is an
oracle upper bound, not a tradeable result. A no-hindsight variant (direction = sign of the 16:30–17:30 net) is reported
alongside in §5; it is **not** in the cell table.

## 6. Data-quality findings surfaced while building (open, not fixed here)

1. **Contract splice in `v9_bars_5min_woodies` on 2026-06-09 / 06-10 / 06-11.** Bars before the splice carry volume 8–400
   and then jump to 25–65 k with a 55–75 pt close→open gap on one bar (06-09 17:25: prev close 7496 → open 7440.75, volume 150 → 31,290).
   Those "IBs" are 70–124 pt wide and fake. Before the guard, a single 06-09 trade contributed **+120.5 pt (+$602.50)** and flipped
   Variation × OPEN_DRIVE from −$28.75 to +$496.25 — i.e. the June Variation "edge" is a data artifact.
2. **Isolated bad bars** with 24–51 pt close→open jumps at normal volume: 07-24 17:40, 07-28 17:25, 09-25 18:55. Excluded by a
   20-pt consecutive-bar jump guard. (Smaller 9–13 pt jumps on 06-08, 07-20, 07-27, 08-21 were kept.)
3. **`v9_tpo_sessions` CASH `ib_high/ib_low` wrong on 5/75 sessions** (listed in §1) and the early-June rows (06-04: POC 7364 vs
   IB 7570) are garbage. Prior-day **VA widths are 3–9 pt** on most rows (e.g. 7441.75/7443.50/7450.50), POC == VAL on several —
   not a credible 70 % value area. The Normal cell therefore rests on a weak VA source; its 3 trades (all −2 pt stops) should
   not be read as evidence about Normal days.
4. Weekend label rows (§0) and 2026-06-03/04 rows without bars.
