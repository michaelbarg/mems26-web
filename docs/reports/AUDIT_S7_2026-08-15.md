## 1. What S7 is

`backend/v9/systems/system7_score.py` — a pure module. Computes a 0-100 confluence score
per setup and maps it to size:

| score | verdict |
|---|---|
| `< 40` | **blocked** (sizing 0) |
| `40-64` | 1 contract |
| `65-84` | 2 contracts |
| `>= 85` | 3 contracts |

**Two consumers:**

| consumer | file:line | passes `market_context`? | passes `bar_ts`? | gated by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gateway (would size/block) | `backend/v9/gateway/trading_gateway.py:1842-1872` | **yes** (`get_market_context()`) | yes (`now(utc)`) | `SYSTEM7_SCORE_V1` — **OFF** |
| Shadow logger (observability) | `backend/v9/gateway/trading_gateway.py:3276-3283` | **NO** | **NO (`bar_ts=None`)** | `S7_SHADOW_LOG_V1` — **ON** |
| Postmortem report | `backend/v9/services/postmortem/analyzer.py:163-174` | **NO** | yes (`trade.entry_ts`) | always computed |

Flag state verified from `.env` on mac-1: `SYSTEM7_SCORE_V1` **absent → code default `"0"` → OFF**.
`S7_SHADOW_LOG_V1=1`, `TSF_SHADOW_LOG_V1=1`, `MARKET_CONTEXT_V1=1`, `DELTA_FEATURES_V1=1`,
`FIXED_CONTRACTS_4=1`, `SCALE_IN_V1=1`.

**The two paths score differently.** This is the single most important finding in this audit
and it is developed in §5.

**Proof that components 6 and 7 are dead, not merely unlucky:** the shadow `ts` column is

### 2.1 Consequence: the score is near-degenerate

With 6 of 9 components pinned to zero, the reachable score is
`30 (base) + {0|15} (location) + {0|10} (delta)`:

```
distinct scores observed : [30, 40, 45, 55]      <- 4 values, ceiling 55
distinct sizing observed : [0, 1]                <- 2 and 3 contracts UNREACHABLE
```

**Turning the flag on in today's shadow configuration would mean "1 contract on everything,
block ~16%".** Because the gateway applies sizing only downward
(`if _s7_contracts < result.get("contracts", 3)` — `trading_gateway.py:1867`), it would
silently override `FIXED_CONTRACTS_4=1` on every single fire.

### 2.2 A correctness bug in the `location` component

`system7_score.py:96-102` scopes the range query to **today**:

```sql
WHERE (ts AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date = (now() AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date
```

`now()` — not the scored bar's date. The component is therefore only valid when scoring in
**real time**. Every retrospective consumer computes location against *today's* range for a
*historical* trade: the postmortem analyzer (`analyzer.py:163`) and any replay/backfill
(`scripts/replay_s7_acceptance.py`). Those scores are silently wrong, and wrong in a way that
looks plausible. This must be fixed before any backtest is used as evidence.

### 2.3 Correction to a prior claim

The audit brief flagged prior-day VAH/VAL from `v9_tpo_sessions` as "DEAD since 2023-11-25".
On mac-1 that is **not the case**: `v9_tpo_sessions` has 123 rows, `max(trading_date) =
2026-08-14`, 121/123 rows carry `vah_price`. Separately, **S7 does not read `v9_tpo_sessions`
at all** — no component sources prior-day value. So this is neither a live dependency nor a
dead one; it is a non-dependency.

## 4. Which component actually carries the signal

Because only two components vary, the score decomposes cleanly:

| split | n | wins | WR | total $ | avg $/trade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `location = 15` (mid-range entry) | 6 | 5 | **83.3%** | **+276.88** | +46.15 |
| `location = 0` (chase / at extreme) | 14 | 3 | 21.4% | -635.00 | -45.36 |
| | | | | | |
| `delta = 10` ("delta confirms") | 11 | 3 | **27.3%** | **-492.50** | -44.77 |
| `delta = 0` (no confirmation) | 9 | 5 | **55.6%** | **+134.38** | +14.93 |

**The delta component is inverted in this sample.** Setups where delta "confirmed the
extension" won 27% of the time and lost $492; setups without confirmation won 56% and made
$134. S7 awards **+10 points** for the condition associated with losing.

The 2×2 makes the damage explicit:

| cell | score | n | wins | WR | total $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| loc=15, delta=0 | 45 | 5 | 5 | **100%** | +355.63 |
| loc=15, delta=10 | 55 | 1 | 0 | 0% | -78.75 |
| loc=0, delta=10 | **40 (passes)** | 10 | 3 | 30% | **-413.75** |
| loc=0, delta=0 | 30 (blocked) | 4 | 0 | 0% | -221.25 |

The worst-performing cohort by dollars (`loc=0, delta=10`, -$413.75) **scores 40 and passes**,
purely because delta lifted it over the 40 threshold. Meanwhile the best cohort scores 45.

**The composite is worse than its own best component:**

| rule | pass n | pass WR | block n | block WR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S7 score (pass `>=40`) | 16 | **50%** | 4 | 0% |
| location alone (pass `loc=15`) | 6 | **83%** | 14 | 21% |

Adding delta to location **destroys** the separation location provides. This is the strongest
single argument against enabling S7 as currently weighted: it is not that the score is noisy,
it is that one of its two live components is pointed the wrong way and is diluting the other.

## 5. Counterfactuals

### 5.1 Sizing — S7 vs the fixed rule

All figures normalized per-contract from realized shadow P&L, resolved rows only (n=20):

| sizing rule | P&L | vs actual |
|---|---|---|
| **actual realized** (mixed 2/3/4c as traded) | **-358.12** | — |
| **S7 recommendation** (block→0c, else 1c) | **+20.63** | **+378.75** |
| flat 1 contract | -75.62 | +282.50 |
| flat 2 contracts | -151.25 | +206.87 |
| flat 3 contracts | -226.87 | +131.25 |
| flat 4 contracts (`FIXED_CONTRACTS_4` reference) | -302.49 | +55.63 |

**S7 vs flat-4: +$323.12. S7 vs flat-1: +$96.25.**

**Read this honestly.** The sample is net-losing (-$358 over 20 trades, 40% WR). On a
net-losing sample **any** reduction in size shows a "gain" — flat-1 alone recovers $282 of the
$379. Of S7's $378.75 improvement over actual, **$282.50 (75%) is size reduction that any
constant 1-contract rule would have delivered**, and only **$96.25 (25%) is attributable to
the block decision**. S7 is not demonstrating selection skill here; it is mostly demonstrating
that trading smaller during a losing week loses less. That is not an edge, and it would
reverse sign in a winning week.

### 5.2 Blocking — would the block verdicts have saved or cost money?

| group | n | wins | WR | realized $ | avg $/contract | avg R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BLOCKED** by S7 (`<40`) | 4 | 0 | **0.0%** | **-221.25** | -24.06 | -1.00 |
| **PASSED** by S7 (`>=40`) | 16 | 8 | 50.0% | -136.87 | +1.29 | -0.07 |

Blocked trades: `(649, LOSS, -86.25) (679, LOSS, -45.00) (686, LOSS, -45.00) (687, LOSS, -45.00)`.
**All four were losers. On the shadow book, blocking saves +$221.25 — and costs nothing,
because it blocked no winner.**

That is the most favourable fact S7 has. Two qualifications:

**(a) It is not statistically significant.** Fisher exact, one-sided (blocked no better than
passed): **p = 0.1022**. Four consecutive losses out of four is exactly what a coin flip
produces ~10% of the time at this base rate. It does not clear 0.05.

**(b) On real money the saving is smaller.** Only 2 of the 4 blocked shadow rows had a
corresponding LIVE trade (matched on id+1 and direction):

| blocked shadow trade | live twin | live outcome | real $ saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| 649 | 650 | LOSS | **+86.25** |
| 679 | 680 | LOSS | **+45.00** |
| 686 | *(none)* | — | 0 |
| 687 | *(none)* | — | 0 |
| | | **total** | **+131.25** |

**Realized live saving would have been $131.25, not $221.25** — against a live book that ran
-$142.50 over 13 trades in the same window.

### 5.3 A caveat that applies to every number above

The outcomes joined to S7 rows are the **shadow trades' simulated outcomes**, not live fills.
Where both exist they frequently diverge: #656 shadow +$87.50 vs live #657 +$73.75;
#659 shadow +$52.50 vs live #660 +$12.50; #663 shadow +$71.25 vs live #664 +$140.00;
#651 shadow LOSS -$78.75 vs live #652 **PHANTOM $0**. The shadow book lost $358 over the same
window in which the live book lost $142.50. **S7 has been graded against a simulator, not
against the broker.**

## 6. Sample sufficiency — plainly

**It is not enough to rule on. Not close.**

```
rows                : 25   (20 resolved, 5 PARTIAL/unresolved)
sessions            : 5    (and 16 of 25 rows come from one session)
block arm           : 4 resolved  = 20% of volume
separation p-value  : 0.1022  (needs < 0.05)
score resolution    : 4 discrete values; 2 of 4 sizing tiers unreachable
live coverage       : 0 of 13 live trades
```

Power requirement for a two-proportion test at α=0.05 / power=0.80, at the observed rate of
**5.0 rows/session**:

| effect to detect | n per arm | total | sessions at current rate | sessions to fill the *block* arm (20% share) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25pt gap (30% vs 55%) | ~58 | ~116 | **~23** | **~58** |
| 20pt gap (35% vs 55%) | ~93 | ~186 | ~37 | ~93 |
| 15pt gap (40% vs 55%) | ~165 | ~330 | ~66 | ~165 |

**But that table is optimistic to the point of being misleading**, because it assumes the
current logger is measuring the right thing. It is not. More sessions of the logger as wired
today can never produce a `60-79` or `80+` row, can never exercise `day_align`, `leg`,
`opening_conf`, `noon_penalty` or `late_zlr`, and can never observe a live trade. **The clock
does not start until the wiring is fixed** (§7) — and fixing it invalidates the existing 25
rows as a baseline, because the scoring function changes.

**Minimum decisive sample, post-fix:** ≥60 resolved rows with ≥20 in the block arm **and**
live-mode coverage, spanning ≥15 distinct sessions with no single session contributing >25%.
At 5 rows/session that is **~4-6 weeks of live logging**. The only way to compress it is a
replay/backtest over historical bars — `scripts/replay_s7_acceptance.py` exists for this — but
**that path is blocked until the `now()` bug in §2.2 is fixed**, or the replay will score every
historical setup against today's range.

## 9. Recommendation

### **FIX-THEN-SHADOW** — do not enable `SYSTEM7_SCORE_V1`.

**Numeric justification:**

1. **The evidence does not describe the thing being authorized.** 6 of 9 components are
   constant-zero in every row (0/25 non-zero for `day_align`, `leg`, `opening_conf`,
   `noon_penalty`, `late_zlr`, `s2_s4_confluence`). The gateway path passes the arguments the
   shadow path omits, and `MARKET_CONTEXT_V1=1` is live — so flag-ON scores would differ from
   every recorded row. **W3 alone disqualifies the current shadow set as ignition evidence.**
2. **Enabling today would clamp every trade to 1 contract.** Ceiling is 55; the 2c tier starts
   at 65 and the 3c tier at 85. Observed sizing values: `[0, 1]`. This silently overrides
   `FIXED_CONTRACTS_4=1`.
3. **One of the two live components is pointed backwards.** `delta=10` → 27.3% WR / -$492.50;
   `delta=0` → 55.6% WR / +$134.38. It drags the worst dollar cohort (`loc=0,delta=10`,
   -$413.75, 30% WR) above the 40-point pass line. The composite scores **50%** WR on passes
   where `location` alone scores **83%** — S7 is currently *subtracting* value from its own
   best input.
4. **The one favourable result is not significant and is inflated.** Blocked 0/4 wins looks
   perfect, but Fisher one-sided **p = 0.1022**, and only 2 of the 4 had live twins → real
   saving **$131.25**, not $221.25.
5. **Most of the apparent sizing "gain" is not skill.** Of +$378.75 vs actual, +$282.50 (75%)
   is what a flat 1-contract rule delivers on a net-losing sample; only +$96.25 is the block
   decision.
6. **Sample:** 25 rows / 5 sessions / 20 resolved / 4 blocked, 64% from a single session, and
   **0 of 13 live trades covered.**
