## 1. The doctrine rule set — stated BEFORE any outcome was looked at

This is the whole anti-hindsight discipline: the rules are fixed, they consume only
start-of-window facts, and they are applied identically to all 24 windows. Where two rules
conflict and the start-of-window facts cannot separate them, the window is marked
**UNDETERMINED**.

**W1 — knowable at 16:30:** prior RTH close / high / low / VA (POC, VAH, VAL), today's open,
the gap. Nothing about IB (it is still forming). The call is therefore a **conditional plan
with both branches fixed in advance**, evaluated against the first 30 minutes.

* **R1 — open beyond the prior day's range (gap).** The reference is the prior extreme
  (PDH or PDL). *If price trades back through it → the gap is rejected: trade toward prior VA
  edge, then prior POC; stop beyond the opening extreme. If the reference holds → trade with
  the gap; stop just inside it; first target = open + one gap width (measured move).*
* **R2 — open inside prior value.** Balance. **No initiative trade.** Responsive only at
  prior VAL/VAH. Open at mid-value → **no trade**.
* **R3 — open outside prior value but inside prior range.** Return-to-value setup: responsive
  toward prior POC, stop beyond the prior extreme — *unless* price accepts outside prior value
  in the first 30 min, in which case trade with acceptance toward the prior extreme.
* **R4 — gap so large that no prior reference is within reach (> ~50% of prior range and
  > 40 pts from PDH/PDL).** **No trade** until the opening range builds its own reference.

**W2 — knowable at 17:30:** locked IB high/low/width, IB width vs the trailing median
(computed from the same bar source, prior sessions only), where W1 closed inside the IB,
whether the gap filled, W1 value vs prior value.

* **R5 — NARROW IB (≤ 0.8× median).** The day has not expressed itself → expect a large
  extension. **Trade the IB break**, stop back inside the IB, first target = 1.0 × IB width
  beyond the break. Direction = the side W1's close leans to; if the close sits at 45–55%
  of the IB, the **direction is UNDETERMINED** (the action — trade the break, do not fade it,
  do not enter inside the IB — still stands).
* **R6 — WIDE IB (≥ 1.4× median) and the gap has filled.** The move already happened.
  **No initiative entry.** Responsive only, toward the IB midpoint / developing POC.
* **R7 — WIDE IB but the gap did NOT fill and value is migrating in the gap's direction.**
  An unfilled gap is one-timeframe control and overrides the wide-IB caution. Trade **with**
  the migration on the extension beyond the IB edge; stop back inside at W1's VA edge;
  first target = IB edge + 0.5 × IB width.
* **R8 — AVERAGE IB (0.8–1.4×).** Normal-Variation expectation: one-sided extension of
  0.5–1.0 IB width. Trade the **first** extension in the direction W1's close leans to;
  stop inside the IB; first target = IB edge + 0.5 × IB width.

**W3 — knowable at 21:00:** IB, the midday extension, POC migration W1→W2, where price sits
vs the developing VA, distance to the session extremes.

* **R9 — price inside the developing VA.** Balance, no location edge → **no trade**.
* **R10 — value migrated ≥ 0.5 × IB during the midday and price is at the day's extreme in
  that direction.** Hold / trail. A **new** entry only on a pullback into the developing VA
  edge or on **acceptance** beyond a named reference — never a fresh breakout chase.
* **R11 — price beyond the developing VA but the POC did NOT migrate (|shift| < 0.1 × IB).**
  Look-above / look-below is unresolved at 21:00 → **UNDETERMINED**.

**Undetermined count: 3 of 24 windows** — 07-30 W3 (R11), 08-06 W2 direction (R5 tie,
close at exactly 50% of IB), 08-04 W3 (**no data** — feed dead). So 2 doctrine-undetermined
+ 1 data-undetermined.

**What the "$ difference" figures are and are not.** For every determinate call the entry
level, stop and first target were fixed by the rule, then replayed bar-by-bar
(`verify_paths.py`, arm on entry touch → first of stop / target). They are a **rule replay**:
no slippage, no queue, one entry per window, and — decisively — **the gate chain returns at
the first blocker, so ~20 gates never evaluated these setups. Nothing here says "it would have
entered."** It says: this is what the doctrine named, and this is what the tape did to it.

## 4. Data-integrity findings surfaced by this audit

1. **2026-08-04: the bar feed dies at 19:50 IL.** 41 of 78 RTH bars. W3 is unauditable, and
   `classify_replay('2026-08-04')` returns `n_bars: 41` — **the `Trend_Normal` EOD label for
   that session was computed on a truncated session.** Any study that treats 08-04 as a
   complete Trend day is standing on 53% of the tape.
2. **`v9_bars_5min` is empty across the slice** (1 row total 08-03→08-05). The woodies table
   is the sole bar source; any CVD/delta work in this period has no source.
3. **`v9_tpo_sessions` CASH IB is NOT shifted** — it matches the bar-derived IB to the tick on
   all 8 dates. The 3-hour shift trap applies to `v9_tpo_history`, not to the IB that
   `classify_replay` consumes. Worth correcting in the standing warning.
4. **`contracts` = 0 on #640** while `pnl_sierra` = −$131.25 implies exactly 3 contracts.
   Any per-contract statistic that reads this field is wrong on this row.
5. **`exit_price` is the last leg only.** On rows with T1/T2 partials
   (#586, #625, #633, #637) the entry→exit points and `pnl_sierra` disagree by design —
   #586 shows −13.50 pts but +$2.50; #637 shows +0.25 pts but +$18.75. Points arithmetic is
   valid only on full stop-outs.
6. **Target ordering artifacts:** #586 has `t1 7515.25 > t3 7491.00 > t2 7481.50`;
   #536 has a non-tick `t1 7511.875` (the known T-277 record artifact).
7. **#652 is a row that is not a trade** — `entry_price` NULL, 0 contracts,
   `CMD_NEVER_SENT_P0-1`. It counts toward "trades per day" in every naive query.
8. **5 of 32 live trades entered with `day_type_at_entry` NULL**, all of them in W1 — the
   label is not yet available in the window where the doctrine most needs it.
