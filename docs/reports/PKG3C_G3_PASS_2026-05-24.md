## 6 · Design observations (informational, all non-blocking)

1. **Import-time invariant enforcement.** Sum-check at module load is the
   right choice — fails immediately on import, not at first lookup. If a
   future amendment breaks the invariant, every test that imports the
   module fails loudly. (No action required.)

2. **`get_contract_split` raises on unknown pattern.** Aligns with pre-LIVE
   protocol "no silent failures". When Pkg 6 (TradeManager) consumes
   percentages, an unrecognized PatternName will surface as a `ValueError`
   at trade emission rather than a 0.0 sizing artifact downstream.
   (Stream 3b-2 + Pkg 6 G3 should test ValueError propagation.)

3. **Default row (Bulkowski 50/30/20) intentionally unmapped.** D-091 row 1
   is the catch-all if no specific family matches; since the codebase
   doesn't emit a "default" pattern_name today, omitting it is correct.
   If Pkg 5d-z later introduces a Bulkowski-modal pattern, add the key
   explicitly (do not fall back). (Documentation pointer for future packages.)

4. **`T1Setup.t3_pct = 0.0` for Flag patterns.** This is the correct
   semantic for "no T3 leg" — Pkg 6 must check `t3_pct > 0` before sizing
   a T3 contract. Defaults of 0.0 on the schema mean any not-yet-mapped
   PatternName would silently emit `0/0/0` if not for `get_contract_split`'s
   ValueError. Defense-in-depth is correct here. (No action required.)

5. **`setup_emitter.emit_t1_setup` adds 7 LOC, no other changes.** Edit
   is surgical and idempotent — re-running `pytest tests/v9/systems/`
   shows no upstream behavior change for emitter callers. (No action required.)
